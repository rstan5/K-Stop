# K-Stop 원스톱

> Your one-stop shop for K-Pop collectibles — powered by AI price verification.

K-Stop is a modern marketplace built for K-Pop fans to safely buy, sell, and trade collectibles with confidence. From rare photocards to official lightsticks, K-Stop uses AI-powered market analysis to determine whether listings are a **Great Deal**, **Fair Price**, or **Above Value** in real time.

No subscriptions. No hidden fees. Just trusted K-Pop trading.

## 🚀 How It Works

### 1. List Your Item
Upload photos and details of your K-Pop merch.

### 2. AI Evaluates
K-Stop analyzes market trends and comparable listings.

### 3. Price Verification
Listings are labeled:
- ✅ Great Deal
- ⚖️ Fair Price
- ⚠️ Above Value

### 4. Easy Checkout
Buy and sell securely with zero monthly fees.
